#About Me  

My name is J.V. Muron and I am currently a junior at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. I am majoring in Mechanical Engineering and minoring in Electrical Engineering.

At the beginning of my college career, I was a student enrolled at Appalachian State University studying Molecular Biology and Forensic Science. My goal was to graduate, become a blood and biological material expert witness, and assist in the cases that dealt with the gathering of evidence from crime scenes and techniques used to preserve biological material. During my sophomore year, I realized I was not interested more in the practice of forensic science, but solving the problems of how and why we use certain techniques and scientific practices. I switched my major to Physics with a concentration in automation and instrumentation. This was my doorway to discovering what Mechanical Engineering actually means. Eventually through my studies, I found that I loved solving how mechanical systems operate and their intersection with electronic devices to automate. This led me to officially transfer to UNCC to pursue my studies of Mechanical and Electrical Engineering. Through my own experiences in Mechanical Engineering, I am very meticulous, detail-orientated, and always striving to deliver the absolute best result that is physically possible. I can and will make mistakes, but I will always correct them and keep moving forward and working hard until the job is done. These skills are what I will carry over into my professional career, and will hopefully see some fruitful results. I look forward to the rest of my time at UNCC, and can not wait for the possibilities and opportunities that will come after.
